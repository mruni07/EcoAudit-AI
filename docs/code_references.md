# EcoAudit AI — Indian Energy Conservation Code References

This document lists the Indian energy conservation codes, standards, and official resources
referenced in the EcoAudit AI application.

> **Disclaimer:** These references are provided for educational purposes only.
> They do NOT constitute legal advice, formal code compliance assessment,
> or a substitute for a qualified professional's inspection.

## Codes and Standards

### 1. Eco Niwas Samhita (ENS) 2018 — Part I: Building Envelope
- **Authority:** Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), Ministry of Power
- **Subject:** Residential building envelope energy conservation
- **Applicability:** Residential buildings; state adoption required for enforcement
- **Official Source:** https://beeindia.gov.in/en/programmes/eco-niwas-samhita

### 2. Eco Niwas Samhita 2021 — Part II: Electro-Mechanical Systems
- **Authority:** BEE, Ministry of Power
- **Subject:** Electro-mechanical and renewable energy systems in residential buildings
- **Applicability:** Residential buildings; state adoption required
- **Official Source:** https://beeindia.gov.in/en/programmes/eco-niwas-samhita

### 3. Energy Conservation and Sustainable Building Code (ECSBC) 2023
- **Authority:** BEE, Ministry of Power
- **Subject:** Integrated energy conservation and sustainability code (successor to ECBC 2017)
- **Applicability:** Primarily commercial buildings; residential provisions for larger complexes
- **Official Source:** https://beeindia.gov.in/en/programmes/energy-conservation-building-codes

### 4. Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC) 2017
- **Authority:** BEE, Ministry of Power
- **Subject:** Commercial building energy conservation (largely superseded by ECSBC 2023)
- **Applicability:** Commercial buildings with connected load ≥100 kW; NOT for individual residences
- **Official Source:** https://beeindia.gov.in/en/programmes/energy-conservation-building-codes

### 5. National Building Code of India (NBC 2016) — SP 7:2016
- **Authority:** Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
- **Subject:** Comprehensive building standards including sustainability provisions
- **Applicability:** National reference standard; local adoption varies
- **Official Source:** https://www.bis.gov.in/

### 6. BEE Standards & Labelling Programme
- **Authority:** BEE, Ministry of Power
- **Subject:** Star rating and labelling for energy-consuming appliances
- **Applicability:** Mandatory for some appliances, voluntary for others
- **Official Source:** https://beestarlabel.com/

### 7. Indian Electricity Rules and Safety Standards
- **Authority:** Central Electricity Authority (CEA) / BIS
- **Subject:** Electrical safety standards for installations
- **Applicability:** Referenced for safety awareness only; not within audit scope
- **Official Source:** https://cea.nic.in/

## Important Notes

1. All codes require state-level adoption for mandatory enforcement.
2. Specific clause numbers and threshold values should be verified from official publications.
3. Code editions may be updated; always check the issuing authority's website for the latest version.
4. This application does not verify compliance with any code or standard.
5. For formal compliance assessment, consult a BEE-certified energy auditor.
